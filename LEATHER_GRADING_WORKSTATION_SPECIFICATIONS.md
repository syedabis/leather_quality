## 3. Component-by-Component Ergonomic & CAD Specs

### 3.1 Overhead Camera Aperture & Mount (Top Roof)
* **Aperture Shape:** Oval / Circular cutout (centered at $X = 50\%$, $Y = 50\%$ on top roof plate).
* **Cutout Dimensions:** $\varnothing 200\text{ mm}$ ($7.87\text{ in}$) or $220 \times 150\text{ mm}$ elliptical.
* **Camera Working Distance (Focal Distance):** **$1,000\text{ mm} - 1,150\text{ mm}$** from inspection bed surface.
* **Camera Field of View (FOV):** Covers a minimum of $1,300 \times 800\text{ mm}$ bed area using an $8\text{ mm} - 12\text{ mm}$ industrial C-mount lens.

### 3.2 Inspection Bed (Slatted Conveyor / Surface)
* **Bed Dimensions:** $1,400\text{ mm Wide} \times 820\text{ mm Deep}$.
* **Slat Spacing:** 5 Slats spaced $120\text{ mm}$ apart center-to-center.
* **Material & Finish:** Non-marking anti-static black polymer (POM/Nylon) or anodized matte aluminum to prevent scratching delicate crust or finished leather.
* **Front Lip Clearance:** $30\text{ mm}$ smooth rounded chamfer to prevent forearm pressure marks on workers.

### 3.3 Light Booth Enclosure (Back & Side Walls)
* **Interior Wall Finish:** Matte Neutral Grey (**Munsell Color System N5 or N7**). Eliminates color reflections and specular glare on glossy leather finishes.
* **Illumination Specification:** Dual high-CRI LED bar strips mounted under the top canopy:
  * **Color Temperature:** $5,000\text{K}$ (Daylight D50/D65 illuminant).
  * **Color Rendering Index (CRI):** $R_a \ge 97$.
  * **Illuminance:** $1,500 - 2,000\text{ Lux}$ uniform illumination at bed surface.

### 3.4 Digital Grade HMI Display Screen (Left Mounting Pillar)
* **Position:** Left outer side wall, angled $15^\circ$ inward toward the operator.
* **Mounting Center Height:** $1,450\text{ mm}$ from floor (human eye-level range: $1,400 - 1,600\text{ mm}$).
* **Screen Specifications:** $10.1\text{-inch}$ or $15.6\text{-inch}$ IP65 industrial touch display with real-time grade indicator (**GRADE A**, **GRADE B**, **REJECT**).

### 3.5 Heavy-Duty Support Stand & Frame Legs
* **Frame Construction:** $40 \times 80\text{ mm}$ or $80 \times 80\text{ mm}$ extruded T-slot aluminum profile or powder-coated structural steel.
* **Standing Height Ergonomics (ISO 14738):**
  * Recommended standing height: **$950\text{ mm}$** (suitable for $5^\text{th}$ to $95^\text{th}$ percentile male and female workers: $900 - 1,050\text{ mm}$).
  * *Optional Motorized Actuator Height Range:* $750\text{ mm} - 1,150\text{ mm}$ (allows seamless transition between seated stool and standing work).
* **Footprint / Base Depth:** $800\text{ mm}$ with heavy-duty vibration-damping rubber leveling feet.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              MAXIMUM FUNCTIONAL REACH ZONE              │
│                     (700 mm Max)                        │
│  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  │

│  │                     (500 mm)                      │  │
│  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
│                      OPERATOR                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Max Reach Distance:** $600\text{ mm} - 700\text{ mm}$ from front desk edge to prevent lumbar spine bending.
2. **Line of Sight Angle:** $15^\circ - 30^\circ$ downward gaze angle from eye level to table center, reducing neck muscle fatigue.
3. **Anti-Fatigue Footing:** Recommends a $15\text{ mm}$ thick industrial polyurethane anti-fatigue floor mat placed in front of the station.
